Inbox Sweeper

A personal desktop app that clears spam and marketing email out of Gmail.

What it does

  • Signs in to your Gmail with "Sign in with Google". It never sees your password.
  • Reads only email headers (sender, subject, date, list headers), never message bodies.
  • Scores each sender with simple rules: unsubscribe headers, bulk-mail flags, noreply addresses, volume.
  • Shows the results grouped by sender so you can move whole senders to Trash, or unsubscribe, in one go.
  • Never permanently deletes anything. "Delete" always means Gmail's Trash.

Privacy in one sentence

The app connects only to Google's own servers, stores everything on your own computer, and sends nothing anywhere else. Full details on the privacy policy page.

Open source

Inbox Sweeper is built with Electron and TypeScript. The source code lives in this repository, so you can read exactly what it does.
