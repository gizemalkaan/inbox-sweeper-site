Inbox Sweeper Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Inbox Sweeper is a desktop application that runs on your own computer. It helps you find and remove spam and marketing email from your Gmail account. This page explains what the app accesses, where that data goes, and how it is protected.

Who operates this app

Inbox Sweeper is a personal, open-source project. There is no company behind it and no server of its own. The app is installed and run by you, on your machine.

What the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app asks for permission to use Gmail over IMAP and SMTP. With that permission it:

  • Reads email headers only: sender, recipient, subject, date, size, and list-management headers such as List-Unsubscribe, Precedence and Auto-Submitted.
  • Reads the recipient addresses of messages in your Sent folder, so senders you have written to are never flagged.
  • Moves messages you select to Gmail's Trash. It never permanently deletes mail.
  • Sends unsubscribe emails on your behalf, only when you click "Unsubscribe" and confirm.

For messages the header rules cannot settle, the app also reads the text of the email, checks it against a fixed list of phrases (for example "unsubscribe" or "order number"), keeps only a few yes/no answers and counts, and discards the text. Attachments and images are never downloaded. Email text is never stored.

Where your data is stored

  • Email headers, scores and the derived text signals (counts and yes/no flags only) are cached in a local SQLite database file in your user profile folder.
  • Your Google sign-in token and the app's own Google client credentials are encrypted with your operating system's secure storage (Windows DPAPI or the macOS Keychain) and saved locally.
  • Settings are stored locally.

Nothing is uploaded to any server operated by the app's author or by any third party. Signing out, or deleting the app's data folder, removes everything.

Network connections

The app connects only to Google:

  • accounts.google.com and oauth2.googleapis.com for Sign in with Google.
  • imap.gmail.com to read headers and message text, and to move mail to Trash.
  • smtp.gmail.com to send unsubscribe emails you have explicitly confirmed.

There is no analytics, no crash reporting, no automatic update check, and no telemetry of any kind. The source code contains a guard that refuses any HTTPS request to a non-Google host.

Use of Google user data

Inbox Sweeper's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used solely to provide the features described above, is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with any third party. No human other than you ever reads it.

Revoking access

You can sign out inside the app, which revokes the token at Google and deletes it locally. You can also remove Inbox Sweeper at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions.
